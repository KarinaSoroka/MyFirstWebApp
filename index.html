My work involves supporting consumer research and fragrance studies. I help with the organisation of studies, consumer data and different activities connected with the research process.

Some of the areas I am involved in include:

  • Supporting fragrance studies
  • Working with consumer research data
  • Coordinating study activities
  • Supporting data analysis and reporting
  • Working with different teams within R&D

Why Consumer Insight Matters

Consumer Technical Insight helps businesses understand how people experience and interact with products. Consumer feedback and data can provide useful information that supports product development and improvement.

From Research to Insight

A simplified consumer research process can include:

  1. Define the research question
  2. Plan the study
  3. Collect consumer feedback
  4. Organise the data
  5. Analyse the results
  6. Identify useful insights
  7. Communicate the findings

Data and Technology

One of the things I find particularly interesting about CTI is the connection between consumers, science and data. Data collected during research can be analysed to help understand consumer experiences and identify patterns in the results.

This also connects with my studies in Computer Science with Data Analytics, where I am developing my technical and data analysis skills.
